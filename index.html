01A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Writer by choice.

I believe the best writing sounds less like a pitch and more like a person.

I’m Saheli Sultana, a freelance writer drawn to the small details that make a story feel true. I work with Indian brands and creative teams to find the heart of an idea—and give it words that sound unmistakably theirs.

From a warm welcome email to a 30-second script, I bring care, curiosity, and a story-first approach to every brief.

04THE GOOD PART

A thoughtful process.
Room for the happy accident.

Good work comes from asking the right questions, staying open, and making the details count.

01 / LISTEN

Start with the why

We talk goals, audience, voice, and what success should feel like. Bring the messy notes; they’re welcome.

02 / FIND

Follow the thread

I dig into your world, gather the useful details, and find the story or angle that feels most true.

03 / SHAPE

Make it feel right

You get a thoughtful first draft, space to react, and a clear revision round to land the language together.

04 / SEND

Ready for the world

We polish the final copy, check the little things, and send it out into the world with confidence.
